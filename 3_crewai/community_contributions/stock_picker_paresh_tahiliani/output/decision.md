## Companies Not Selected

### 1. Arm Holdings (ARM)
* **Market Position & Outlook:** Arm holds a near-monopolistic position in mobile processor architecture and is successfully expanding into hyperscale data centers and custom AI accelerators, backed by rising analyst price targets and aggressive chip licensing.
* **Why Not Selected:** While Arm represents an exceptional long-term growth story in edge computing and custom silicon, its historically high valuation multiples price in a tremendous amount of near-term perfection. Compared to Micron, which offers robust contracted revenue visibility (take-or-pay agreements) at a more compelling forward valuation, Arm carries higher multiple contraction risk if broader market sentiment shifts.

### 2. Accenture (ACN)
* **Market Position & Outlook:** Accenture remains the premier global leader in IT consulting and digital transformation, bolstering its capabilities through strategic partnerships (such as its recent Anthropic deal) and enterprise cloud/AI adoption.
* **Why Not Selected:** Accenture is a solid blue-chip technology consulting investment providing reliable cash flows and steady equity appreciation. However, it operates in a more service-oriented segment facing near-term macroeconomic headwinds and corporate settlement scrutinies. It lacks the explosive, cyclical top-line velocity and direct infrastructure leverage that semiconductor leaders like Micron capture during the current AI boom.
