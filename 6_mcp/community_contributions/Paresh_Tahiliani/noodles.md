# Hakka Noodles Recipe

A popular Indo-Chinese dish made by stir-frying Hakka noodles with vegetables, sauces, and garlic on high heat. 

- **Prep time:** 25 mins
- **Cook time:** 15 mins
- **Total time:** 40 mins
- **Servings:** 2

---

## Ingredients

### Noodles & Base
- **1 pack (5.5 oz / 150 grams)** Hakka noodles (such as Ching's) or egg noodles / noodles of choice
- **2¼ tablespoons** oil (high smoke point oils like peanut, sunflower, avocado, coconut, or refined sesame oil)
- **1 tablespoon** garlic, finely chopped
- **½ tablespoon** ginger, finely chopped (optional)
- **2 to 3** spring onions / green onions (chopped, greens and whites separated)
- **1 small (½ cup / 50 grams)** onion, sliced thin (yellow or red onion)
- **1** green chili, chopped (optional)

### Vegetables
- **1 small (50 grams)** carrot, peeled and julienned
- **½ to ¾ cup (80 grams)** capsicum / bell pepper (green, yellow, or red), julienned
- **6** French beans / green beans, finely chopped
- **½ cup (60 grams)** cabbage, finely shredded (optional)

### Sauce & Seasoning
- **1¼ tablespoons** organic soy sauce (or tamari, use more if desired)
- **1 teaspoon** rice vinegar (substitute with apple cider vinegar)
- **¼ teaspoon** black pepper or white pepper (adjust to taste)
- **¼ to ½ teaspoon** sea salt (adjust to taste)

### Optional Add-ins
- **1 tablespoon** hot sauce or chili garlic sauce
- **½ to 1 tablespoon** chili oil

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Boil the Noodles
1. Pour **8 cups of water** into a large pot and bring to a rapid boil. 
2. Add the noodles and cook until *al dente* (follow the package instructions for exact timing; do not overcook).
3. Drain the noodles into a colander and rinse immediately under cold running water to remove excess starch and stop the cooking process.
4. Add **¼ tablespoon of oil** to the drained noodles and toss well to prevent them from clumping together. Set aside.

### Step 2: Prepare the Sauce Mix
1. In a small bowl, combine the soy sauce, rice vinegar, hot sauce (if using), and chili oil (if using). Keep aside.

### Step 3: Stir-Fry
1. Heat a wok (preferably cast iron or carbon steel) on **high heat**. 
2. Pour in the oil and swirl it to coat the wok evenly.
3. Add the chopped garlic and ginger (if using). Fry for **30 seconds**.
4. Add the spring onion whites, sliced onions, and green chili. Sauté for about **1 minute** until the onions turn translucent.
5. Add the carrots, capsicum, cabbage, and French beans. Stir-fry for **1 to 2 minutes** until the vegetables are partially cooked while maintaining a crisp crunch.
6. Add the prepared noodles, salt, and pour the sauce mixture all over the noodles.
7. Toss everything vigorously and stir-fry for **1 to 2 minutes** on high flame. 
8. Taste test and adjust salt or sauces if needed. Stir in the ground pepper and spring onion greens.

---

## Expert Tips

- **Thin & Uniform Veggies:** Chop the vegetables very thin and uniform so they cook quickly and blend seamlessly with the noodles.
- **Do Not Overcook Noodles:** Cook them *al dente*, rinse immediately with cold water, and toss with oil so they stay non-sticky and bouncy.
- **High Heat & Wok:** Keep the heat high from start to finish. Using a carbon steel or cast iron wok helps impart the signature smoky flavor (*wok hei*).
- **Adequate Oil:** Do not skimp on the oil, as it is essential for the authentic restaurant-style texture and taste.
