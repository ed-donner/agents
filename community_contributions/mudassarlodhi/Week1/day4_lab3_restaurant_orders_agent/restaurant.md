## Ordering Rules

* Customers can place dine-in, takeaway, and delivery orders.
* Delivery is available within a 10 km radius.
* The minimum delivery order is **$15** before taxes and delivery fees.
* Delivery orders normally take **30–60 minutes**.
* During busy periods, delivery may take longer.
* Customers cannot modify an order once it has entered the **Preparing** status.
* Customers may request cancellation before an order enters **Preparing**.
* Cancellation requests after preparation has started are subject to restaurant approval.
* The assistant must never guarantee a cancellation or refund unless the order data explicitly confirms it.
* The assistant must not invent an order number, order status, delivery time, price, customer record, or refund.

---

## Reservation Rules

* Reservations are accepted for parties of up to 8 people.
* Reservations can be made during restaurant opening hours.
* Parties larger than 8 require direct contact with the restaurant.
* A reservation does not guarantee a specific table unless explicitly confirmed by restaurant staff.
* The assistant must not claim that a reservation exists unless reservation data explicitly confirms it.
