# Tesla Model Y vs. Ford Mustang Mach-E for a 100-Car Sales Fleet

## Quick comparison

| Category | Tesla Model Y | Ford Mustang Mach-E |
|---|---|---|
| EPA range | ~277–337 miles depending on trim | ~240–320 miles depending on trim |
| Charging | Tesla Supercharger access; up to 250 kW peak DC fast charging | Up to 150 kW DC fast charging; BlueOval Charge Network access |
| Cargo room | ~34 cu ft behind rear seats; ~76 cu ft max | ~29.7 cu ft behind rear seats; ~59.7 cu ft max |
| Fleet fit | Strong for high-mileage drivers and long route days | Solid, with good range and practical SUV packaging |

## Fleet takeaways

- **Tesla Model Y:** Better range, faster charging, and stronger charging-network coverage make it the more operationally efficient choice for a large sales fleet.
- **Ford Mustang Mach-E:** Competitive range and familiar dealer/service support, but it trails the Model Y on charging speed and cargo capacity.

## Recommendation

**Choose the Tesla Model Y** for the 100-car sales fleet. Its longer range, quicker DC fast charging, and Supercharger access should reduce downtime and make route planning easier for high-mileage sales use. The Mach-E is a credible alternative, but the Model Y is the stronger fleet choice on day-to-day operational efficiency.
