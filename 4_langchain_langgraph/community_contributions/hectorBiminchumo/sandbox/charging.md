# US Public EV Charging Landscape

## Public charging supply
The US public EV charging network has grown quickly in recent years. A reasonable current estimate is **roughly 200,000 public charging points** nationwide, spread across about **70,000 charging locations**. The exact figure varies by source and date because stations are added continuously, but the overall picture is clear: public charging is now a large and expanding national infrastructure. For a fleet planning electrification, that means public charging can increasingly support top-up charging, long-distance travel, and routes that extend beyond depot or home charging.

## Two major networks to rely on
**ChargePoint** is one of the largest and most established charging networks in the US, with a very broad footprint and a mix of AC and DC charging. It is widely used by commercial drivers and fleet operators, and its networked software and roaming access can simplify account management and station discovery.

**EVgo** is another major US network, with a strong focus on fast charging. It is especially useful for fleet vehicles that need quick turnaround times between routes or during off-site duty. EVgo has a sizable national presence and is often positioned as a dependable public fast-charging option in urban and corridor locations.

## Fleet takeaway
For a sales fleet, public charging should be viewed as a supporting layer rather than the sole charging strategy. But with a national network of roughly 200,000 public charge points and major providers like ChargePoint and EVgo, the US market now offers enough scale to support mixed charging plans for many fleet use cases.
