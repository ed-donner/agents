# New York ↔ London round-trip flight recommendations

I searched for a round-trip from New York to London leaving about a month from now and returning a week later. Based on price first, then total journey time, and preferring itineraries with fewer stops, here are the top three options:

## 1) Best overall
- **Airline:** Virgin Atlantic
- **Outbound:** New York (JFK) → London Heathrow (LHR), **Nov 19, 2026**, 6:30 PM → 6:40 AM (+1)
- **Return:** London Heathrow (LHR) → New York (JFK), **Nov 26, 2026**, 9:00 AM → 12:10 PM
- **Stops:** Nonstop both ways
- **Price:** **$448**
- **Why it ranks first:** Lowest price among the best nonstop options, with solid total journey time.

## 2) Runner-up

- **Outbound:** New York (JFK) → London Heathrow (LHR), **Nov 19, 2026**, 8:15 PM → 8:25 AM (+1)

- **Stops:** Nonstop both ways

## 3) Best cheaper-but-slower fallback
- **Airline:** Aer Lingus
- **Outbound:** New York (JFK) → London Heathrow (LHR), **Nov 19, 2026**, 5:20 PM → 10:30 AM (+1)
- **Return:** London Heathrow (LHR) → New York (JFK), **Nov 26, 2026**, 1:20 PM → 6:40 PM
- **Stops:** 1 stop each way
- **Price:** **$399**
- **Why it ranks third:** Cheapest option, but the extra stop makes the journey noticeably longer.

## Recommendation
If you want the best balance of **price first** and then **journey time**, I recommend **Virgin Atlantic at $448**. It is a nonstop itinerary and avoids the time penalty of one- or multi-stop options.
