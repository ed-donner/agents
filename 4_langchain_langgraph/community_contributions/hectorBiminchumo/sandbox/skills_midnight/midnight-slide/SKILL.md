# Midnight blue recommendation slide

## When to use

Use this skill when a briefing or comparison ends in a recommendation and someone asks for a slide in the midnight blue style. The deliverable is always a single slide, never a full deck.

## House style

- Say less. Every word on the slide must earn its place
- The title is a bold statement of the outcome, not a label. Three to five words, no colon, no trailing period, for example "Model Y. Built for fleets." Keep it under 30 characters
- Exactly three key points. Each is a short fragment of eight words or fewer, with one concrete number where possible, for example "330 miles of range" rather than "The vehicle offers a range of about 330 miles"
- The recommendation is one confident sentence in plain language that states the decision. Mention the runner-up only if it fits in the same sentence
- Tone: calm, direct and confident. No jargon, no hedging words such as "might" or "arguably", no exclamation marks, no emoji
- Colors, typography and layout (deep blue background, white headline, pale blue points, one sky blue accent) are applied automatically by the create_midnight_slide tool, so focus on the words and let the tool handle the design

## Workflow

1. Read the briefing or comparison you have been given
2. Find the single idea that matters, then distill the three short key points and the one sentence recommendation
3. Call the create_midnight_slide tool with the title, key points and recommendation
